**Install**
- **Windows x64:** `Kod-Setup-0.13.1.exe`. Kod 0.2.x to 0.13.0 update themselves to this version.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Kod-0.13.1-mac-arm64.dmg` (ad-hoc signed: right-click › Open the first time).
- **Linux x64:** `Kod-0.13.1-linux-x86_64.AppImage`.

Kod needs the **.NET 10 SDK** on PATH.

**Fix: code completion puts the best match first**
- Typing `Con` and pressing Enter now inserts `Console`, not `ConcurrentExclusiveSchedulerPair`. Items that match what you typed equally well are no longer sorted alphabetically.
- Equal matches now go: locals and parameters, members, keywords, types, then namespaces, with shorter names first. `str` picks `string`, and `co` picks a local named `count`.
- The list after a `.` with nothing typed yet is unchanged.
